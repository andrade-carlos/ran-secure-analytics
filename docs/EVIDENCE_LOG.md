## Categories

* **Development** – commits, releases, new features (link the commit or tag).
* **Infrastructure** – cloud account, VM, domain, TLS certificate (invoice / receipt / screenshot).
* **Demo** – live demo given to a prospect (date, organization, attendee role; meeting invite).
* **Outreach** – contact with an ISP/WISP/association (email thread, LinkedIn message, event badge).
* **Letter of Interest** – signed LOI from a provider (PDF in a private folder, not in this repo).
* **Publication** – article, talk, video, white paper (URL).
* **Business** – LLC formation, bank account, contracts, investment received (official documents).

> Keep private documents (letters, invoices, bank statements) **outside** this public repository.
> Store them in a private folder and reference them here only by name and date.
